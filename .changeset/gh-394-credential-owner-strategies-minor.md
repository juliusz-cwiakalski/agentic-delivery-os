Make credential sources declarable over the closed four-style vocabulary
(`literal`, `environment-prefixed`, `command`, `harness-store`): the shipped
closed allowlist for `command` sources with its CI tripwire, store-ownership
resolution over the four injected construction-input seams, the declarable
tracker credential reference (shipped default byte-identical), the live
register/values redaction registry consulted at render time, and identifier-only
`source`/`reason`/`remedy` projection fields forwarded at every reconstruction
site onto every rendered surface. Existing declarations validate and resolve
byte-identically; no operator edit is required.
