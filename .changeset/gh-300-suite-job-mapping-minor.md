`verify.suite-map` declaration read from the project-committed configuration
document through one bounded resolution attempt, the additive
`data.suiteBreadth`/`data.mirrorsJob` report fields on both report types, the
`verify.narrowed-scope` refusal (exit 1, `detail.suiteBreadth`, the pinned
summary header, no verification record and no capsule event), and the two
bounded identifier-only `cause:` lines for an unmirrored or uncorroborated
declaration. The shipped MCP `gate-report` composition supplies the same map
read, the five gates and the durable record and capsule shapes are unchanged,
and this repository declares its own reference map in `.delivery-os/config.yaml`.
