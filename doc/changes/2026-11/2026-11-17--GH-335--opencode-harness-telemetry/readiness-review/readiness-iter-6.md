## Verdict Rationale

Every claim the iteration-5 gate tested now holds. The carrier is fixed where it matters: TDR-0040's amendment A-4 states the two members, their directions and their one-phase landing, and the record's decision statement, Decision 2, implementation step, DM-4 consequence, KISS row and verification criteria all agree with spec DEC-16/F-3/DM-4/AC-F3-1, with the test plan's TC-011/015/047, and with plan Phases 2.1/3.3/4.3/5.6. The rule is falsifiable at the seam where the claim is made: TC-011 and TC-015 supply the agent identity as the caller and assert the resolver's recorded `{agent, profile}` input and identity with its returned value, so a composition that fails to read the member — or a binding assembled without the resolver — fails a test that production code must pass. The absence axis is one classification per condition: `agent-divergence` for a stated-but-different observed agent, the declared marker plus placeholder plus same-axis `optional-field-absent` plus `unresolved-seat` for an unstated one, and `invalid-session-identity` only for a genuinely unusable identity (a reachable control, verified in the delivered extractor). Nothing left states that the shipped `dispatch` proves propagation or that a missing axis yields a fabricated identity.

Feasibility holds on the numbers I re-derived rather than trusted: 47 TCs in three independent forms (headings, index, map) with exact set equality, 27 ACs identical across the three artifacts, 8 phases summing to 47, every gate path existing now or declared in the phase that runs it or an earlier one, every created file inside a declared scope, and no phase needing a port-input member it may not add. Both plan validators are green and plan id hygiene after the TDR renumber is sound.

What remains is four minor precision defects and one drifting observation count. They are real and must be fixed before the PR — most importantly, the adapters-contract record's authoring step (finding 4) must take the reference-only reading so the change does not grow a fourth home for the carrier it spent three iterations making consistent. But none of them changes a contract, makes a criterion unsatisfiable, or leaves a coder unable to execute a phase: the operative statements in every artifact are already correct, and the authoritative record carries the two-member form.

**Delivery may start.** Verdict READY; remediation of findings 1–3 and 5 rides the normal delivery phases (with finding 4 resolved at Phase 1 authoring time), and none of them reopens `specification`, `test_planning` or `delivery_planning` as a gate.
