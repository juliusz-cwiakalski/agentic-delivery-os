[major] spec_completeness / ac_quality / cross_artifact_consistency — `chg-GH-176-spec.md` F-2 (line 160: "Each candidate is evaluated independently against the same shared facts; no candidate blocks or changes another"), §8.3 (lines 281-289), AC-F2-3 (line 473); `chg-GH-176-plan.md` RES-6 (line 103), task 3.2 (line 473); `chg-GH-176-test-plan.md` TC-QREADY-036 steps 5 and 11 (lines 1617, 1623), corpus `identity-ambiguous-suffix` (line 1759).

   Suggested remediation target phase: specification
   Suggested fix: Qualify F-2: condition evaluation is independent per candidate over shared facts, but identity observation may depend deterministically on the requested candidate set through the §8.3 unique-suffix bridge; a shared suffix leaves the item unobserved for every sharing candidate and never assigns it by precedence or caller order. Keep AC-F2-3 unchanged.
